Privacy Policy

YouTube Studio Scheduler ("the app") is a private, single-user tool that its owner uses to schedule uploads to their own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public, and only the owner's Google account can sign in.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data is used and stored

  • Your Google account email and name, to check that you are allowed to sign in.
  • For connected YouTube channels: channel ID, title and handle, and an OAuth refresh token. Tokens are encrypted before they are stored.
  • Videos you upload, held temporarily in Google Cloud Storage and deleted after upload, plus their metadata (title, description, tags, schedule) and the resulting YouTube video IDs.

How it is used and shared

Data is used only to upload and schedule videos on the owner's channels, keep an optional Google Sheet in sync, and email the owner a summary. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Video metadata may be sent to Anthropic's Claude API to draft titles and descriptions.

Retention and deletion

Staged video files are deleted after upload. You can revoke the app's access at any time at Google security settings; stored tokens then stop working and can be deleted on request.
